Announcement

Email report setup is now available

Admins can set up attendance reports by adding a sender email, entering that email's app password, and saving the report receiver addresses in the Admin Dashboard.

  • The sender email automatically receives a copy of every report.
  • Manual reports can be sent right away from the dashboard.
  • Automatic reports send on the schedule you choose after setup is complete.
