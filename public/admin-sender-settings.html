App Password Help (Simple Version)

Important: Use an App Password, not your regular email password.

Gmail

  1. Sign in to Gmail.
  2. Go to Google Account → Security.
  3. Turn on 2-Step Verification.
  4. Click App Passwords.
  5. Create a new password for Mail.
  6. Copy the password and paste it here.
  7. If the password has spaces, remove the spaces.

Outlook / Microsoft

  1. Sign in to your Microsoft account at account.microsoft.com.
  2. Go to Security and then open Advanced security options.
  3. Turn on Two-step verification if it is not already enabled.
  4. Under App passwords, click Create a new app password.
  5. Copy the generated app password.
  6. Paste the app password into the sender app password field here and save.

AOL

  1. Sign in to AOL.
  2. Open Account Security.
  3. Click Generate App Password.
  4. Choose Mail.
  5. Copy the password, and make sure no spacing.

Yahoo

  1. Sign in to Yahoo.
  2. Open Account Security.
  3. Click Generate App Password.
  4. Choose Mail.
  5. Copy the password and paste it here.

Other Email Providers

  1. Search for "App Password" or "SMTP Password" for your email provider.
  2. If you cannot find one, contact your email provider for SMTP settings.
  3. Use your email address and the app password here.
